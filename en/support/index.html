Help & FAQ

Answers and contact details

How do I complete a habit?

Tap the circular checkmark on the right side of a habit. It leaves Today’s pending list and appears in your statistics.

Why didn’t I receive a reminder?

Check “Settings → Notification Permission,” then open “iPhone Settings → Notifications → HabitSum.” Focus modes and Notification Summary can also delay delivery.

Where are records stored?

The current version stores habits and completions locally. They are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. You can export all records from Settings.

How do I restore Pro?

Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase and tap “Restore Purchases” on the Pro screen. A non-consumable lifetime purchase does not expire.

Will records restore on a new phone?

The current version does not provide iCloud data sync. Your Pro purchase can be restored, but local habit records are not restored with it. Export before changing devices.

How do I delete data?

Swipe left on a habit to delete it and its related records. Uninstalling the App removes all App data stored on that device.
