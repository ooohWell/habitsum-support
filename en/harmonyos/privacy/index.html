HarmonyOS Privacy Policy

Version date: September 25, 2026 · Pre-release draft

Scope and data controller

This policy applies only to HabitSum for HarmonyOS (bundle name com.yangwen.habitsum), not the iOS version.

Data handled on your device

Habit names and descriptions, schedules, icons and colors, reminder times, check-ins, reflections, and your language, appearance, and introduction preferences are stored in the app's local storage. They are used to show your habits, calculate statistics, and retain your settings. The current version requires no account and does not upload this content to a developer-operated server.

Reminders and haptics

You can use habit tracking without adding reminders. If you configure reminders, allow notifications, and the system supports the required reminder capability, the app registers the reminder time and habit name with HarmonyOS so the system can show a notification when due. You can remove reminders in the app or turn off notifications in system settings. The app also invokes the system vibration capability for check-in feedback; it does not read sensor data.

Import, export, and sharing

Only when you choose “Export all data” does the app write your habits, check-ins, and reflections to a file location you select. That file contains content you entered, so keep it secure. For import, the app reads only the JSON file you select through the system file picker and merges new items on this device. A copy you share or upload elsewhere is governed by that recipient's practices.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local data remains while you use the app. You can view, edit, archive, or delete a habit; deletion also removes its check-ins and reflections. You can export a copy. Uninstalling removes data in the app's local storage, but any file you exported must be deleted separately from its saved location. This version has no developer account or in-app cross-device sync. Whether system backup includes app data depends on your device and system settings.

Third parties, purchases, and children

The current HarmonyOS version has no advertising, third-party analytics, or account SDK, and no in-app purchasing integration. It uses HarmonyOS notification, vibration, and file-selection capabilities. This support site itself loads no analytics scripts or third-party fonts. If purchasing, cloud sync, or other data processing is added later, we will update this policy and provide any required in-app notice first.

The app is not specifically directed at children and offers no public posting or social features.

Changes

Material changes will be published here with an updated date. You may email us to ask about data processing or request help accessing, copying, correcting, or deleting your information.
