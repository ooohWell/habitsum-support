HarmonyOS Help

Frequently asked questions

How do I check in?

Tap the checkmark on the right of a habit card in Habits. Open a card to see its history and edit it. Tapping a reminder notification opens the habit; it does not check in automatically.

Why didn't I receive a reminder?

Make sure the habit has a reminder time, and allow notifications both in HabitSum's Settings and in HarmonyOS system settings. The system must also grant the reminder-agent capability; reminders may not register until it does. Silent, Do Not Disturb, and power-saving settings can affect delivery.

On which days will I be reminded?

Daily habits use the chosen time every day. Weekly habits use the same time on each selected weekday. A second time adds another reminder on those days. Monthly, yearly, and specific-date habits are scheduled only for their configured dates.

How do I back up or move my records?

Habits, check-ins, and reflections are stored locally by default. This HarmonyOS version has no in-app cloud sync. Before changing devices, use Settings → Export all data to save a JSON file, then select that file with Import data on the new device.

How do I delete data?

Swipe left on a habit in Habits or History to archive or delete it. Archiving keeps its history; deleting removes its check-ins and reflections. Uninstalling removes local app data. Files you exported must be deleted separately from their saved location.

How many habits can I create?

The current HarmonyOS version is free to use, with no habit-count limit or purchase required.
