Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 23, 2026

Overview

HabitSum (“the App”) is a local-first habit tracker. We respect your privacy, do not sell personal information, and do not use your habit content for advertising or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Habits, completions, reflections, reminder times, icons, and colors are stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive or read this content through a developer-operated server.

HabitSum does not provide iCloud data sync. If iCloud device backup is enabled in iOS, the App’s local data may be included in your device backup. This process is controlled by Apple and your system settings, and the developer cannot access the contents of that backup.

Notifications

The App schedules local notifications only after you grant permission and only for reminders you configure. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

HabitSum Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It is not a subscription and never auto-renews. Apple processes the purchase through StoreKit; the developer does not receive your card number or full payment details. The App only reads the transaction status supplied by Apple to determine whether Pro is unlocked.

Export and deletion

You can export all local records from Settings. Deleting a habit also deletes that habit’s completions and reflections. Uninstalling the App removes all App data stored on that device, so export first if you want a backup.

Third parties and collection

The current version contains no advertising SDK, creates no user account, does not yet offer iCloud sync, and does not send habit content to third parties or use it for advertising or tracking. This support site loads no cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party fonts. If this changes, we will update this policy and request any permission required by the platform.

Children’s privacy

The App is not designed to collect children’s personal information and provides no public social or user-content publishing features.

Changes

Material changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
